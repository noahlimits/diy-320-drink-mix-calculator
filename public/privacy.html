Privacy Policy

DIY 320 Drink Mix Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

The app does not require user registration and does not request access to location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, or other device permissions.

Values entered into the app are used only to perform calculations on the user's device. They are not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling technologies.

Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no personal data retained by the developer or available for deletion.

The app is not directed to children.

Privacy inquiries may be submitted using the developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.
